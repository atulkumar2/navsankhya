If you are looking for a place to donate, I recommend **The Akshaya Patra Foundation**.

The foundation provides meals to children in government and government-aided schools through its school meal programme. You can learn about its work on the [official Akshaya Patra website](https://www.akshayapatra.org/).

## How to contribute

Visit the [official donation page](https://www.akshayapatra.org/onlinedonations) to review the available options and donate directly to the foundation.
